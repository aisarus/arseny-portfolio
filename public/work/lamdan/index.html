Lamdan

How do you make generated study material useful without pretending that generated material is authoritative?

Product direction

Lamdan evolved from a generic study-room concept toward a source-linked academic workspace. The important idea is not generation for its own sake: AI drafts remain reviewable, source provenance stays visible, and persistence/backup/rollback/cancellation are treated as product behavior rather than afterthoughts.

What it demonstrates

Product reframing, trust boundaries around generated content, multilingual/local search concerns, browser E2E thinking and the ability to redirect an AI-built product when the first shape is not good enough.

Status

This is a prototype and evolving project, not presented as a finished production learning platform. Its value in the portfolio is the product reasoning and implementation path.
